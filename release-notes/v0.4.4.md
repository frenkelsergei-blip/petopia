# PETOPIA 0.4.4 · Foxy's new animations

- My Pet: Foxy eats from its own bowl (head in the bowl, chewing, licking, a happy heart) while the child kneels and watches.
- Bath time: Foxy splashes in a little tub with bubbles, then shakes dry.
- Sleep: Foxy curls up in bed and breathes slowly, and stretches when it wakes up.
- Catch the Ball: Foxy runs back with the sun ball in its mouth.
- Crates stay in your bag until you choose "Place a crate" in the bag; no more see-through crate following you.
- The river bridge is a real drawbridge now: it swings down on ropes and lands with a bounce.
- Fixed thin dark lines that could appear along platforms and objects on phones, and the cave wall that went dark when you walked.
