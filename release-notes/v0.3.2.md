# PETOPIA 0.3.2 · Foxy finds things

- The Use button now says what it does: Enter, Open, Read, Pick up or Place, each with its own picture.
- When Foxy smells something hidden, a little dirt mound sparkles and Find turns into Dig. Foxy digs up crates and coins.
- Crates go into your bag. Place them to stand on and reach high ledges, and pick them up again. There is a new robot part only crates can reach.
- The bag shows your crates and robot parts.
- Double Jump is explained with a card when you learn it.
