# PETOPIA 0.3.8 · Foxy grows up with you

- Baby Foxy is now small next to you in the adventure, and grows as Foxy gets older.
- Take care of Foxy before exploring: a hungry or sleepy Foxy stays home and tells you what's needed.
- Exploring makes Foxy hungry and tired. A tired Foxy can't dig or help with Double Jump. Give a snack from your bag or rest at a lantern.
- A grown-up Foxy smells treasure from farther away and digs faster.
- A small Foxy badge at the top shows food and energy.
