# PETOPIA 0.4.8 · Your settings, a deeper cave

- Settings in sections: Sound (music and effects volume, voices), Screen and help (minimap, hint bar, next goal on the map, place names, reduced motion), Touch controls (button and joystick size, spacing, height, see-through, left-handed) and Play (gentle mode: no hearts lost). Reset all settings in one tap.
- The main menu's Settings has an "All settings" button to the full list.
- The Hidden Cave has real depth: a far cavern, distant arches, crystal pillars and a dark foreground, each moving at its own speed.
- Falling in the water is smooth now: one splash, a short fade, and you stand back on firm ground away from the edge (no more flicking back and forth).
