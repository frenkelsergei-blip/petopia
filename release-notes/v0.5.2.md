# PETOPIA 0.5.2 · Foxy holds the button

- Windmill Cellar: stand by the glowing button and tap Sit. Foxy sits on it and holds the stone gate open while you go through to the treasure. Tap Come to call Foxy back. (A crate still works too.)
- Foxy keeps up much better: runs faster when behind, no longer waits at old spots while you jump, and pops in right behind you (never ahead) when it has to catch up.
- When Foxy finds something and you walk on past, it lets it go and follows you.
- The Sky Feather flies to your bag as a complete, glowing feather.
