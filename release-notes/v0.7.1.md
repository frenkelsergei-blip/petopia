# PETOPIA 0.7.1 · Hand-drawn animation

- The explorer and Foxy now move with hand-drawn paper poses played frame by frame (run, take-off, rise, fall, landing, idle), feet on the ground, steps matched to the distance moved.
- Pet room: Foxy sized by age, bowls apart near the front, duck in the tub, bath and sleep in the bed.
- Checkpoints are paper flags that rise when you reach them; no street lamps; the blurry close-up layer is gone.
- Calmer three-layer background with more depth between layers.
- The river bridge is a pop-up drawbridge folding down from the bank.
- Bigger joystick with a lighter touch for left and right.

Validation: all 15 automated suites pass. Still open: settings and other menus from their new concepts, a less crowded world (new concepts), stone-breaking and critter animations.
