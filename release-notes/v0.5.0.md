# PETOPIA 0.5.0 · Foxy helps you find the way

- Foxy is a real helper now: when you come near something hidden, Foxy runs to the spot, barks and shows a "!". Go there and press Dig. Foxy can dig up the golden key, snacks, jump carrots, crates and coins, in every place.
- A golden guide arrow at the edge of the screen shows the way to your next goal ("To River Crossing"), and a bouncing arrow marks the spot when it's on screen. Turn it off in Settings once you know the way.
- New status card at the top left: Foxy's portrait, your hearts and Foxy's food and energy. The place name shows when you arrive.
- Minimap and hint bar are off by default (turn them on in Settings).
- Softer, gentler sounds everywhere: soft digging, "nom nom" eating, wooden button taps, calmer music. Footsteps and voices are off.
- Smoother scrolling: the view and the background layers move together (no shaking when jumping).
- New doors built into their walls: the tower archway, the locked tower door, and a secret paw-stone door in the hillside that slides into the ground.
- Crumbling stones break into pieces with dust; landing, digging, chests and coins have little puffs and sparkles. Coin counter fits big numbers.
