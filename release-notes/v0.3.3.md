# PETOPIA 0.3.3 · A real adventure

- You can always see where the path goes: wooden arches with signs at the edges of each place, a cave entrance, and a tower door. Climb the Old Tower to reach the Forest Gate.
- Three hearts. Watch out for thorns, water and grumpy hedgehogs (jump on them!). If you run out of hearts, Foxy helps you back to the last lantern and you keep everything you found.
- Light the lanterns as you go: they refill your hearts and become your return point.
- A log raft to ride over the stream, crumbling stones in the cave, and a windmill turning in the fields.
- New map button: see where you have been, where you are, and which places still hide a chest.
