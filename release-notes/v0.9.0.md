# PETOPIA 0.9.0 · Torn-paper screens everywhere

- Every screen is rebuilt from its own torn-paper concept (P40–P58): the adventure HUD, main menu, pause and settings, My Pet treehouse with its care board, food chooser and room menu, bag, shop, Adventures map, Foxy details, adoption, adventure result, Catch the Ball, the rotate notice, collection, mini games, the area map, the new-ability card, coin toasts and decorating. Phones held upright get their own layouts.
- HUD as in the concept: bigger status card with hearts, Food/Energy bars and icons, smaller coin counter, round buttons with white rings and soft shadows, captions with a white outline, a cleaner joystick, and the place name always shown on a soft-white banner.
- My Pet is the new treehouse room with a leaf bed, stone bath and bowls; the explorer and Foxy are bigger in it.
- Soft paper shadows for every animal and object (no more hard dark copies). Hedgehogs, crabs and frogs walk and hop with real animation.
- Foxy keeps up with you: it lets go of a dig spot sooner and catches up. Its gallop is smoother and less hurried.

Still in progress: Metroidvania level layouts, the room makeover shop (Decorate shows the coming items), more animation polish.
