# PETOPIA 0.3.6 · Smoother adventure

- Bigger movement stick that no longer gets stuck.
- Start and Continue take you straight into Green Meadows.
- Turn the big wheel to lower the River Crossing bridge.
- The Old Tower is a real climb now: crumbling steps, a moving lift, thorns and a hedgehog. From the top you step out onto a high ledge at the Forest Gate.
- Signs have words on them, doors show a bouncing arrow, and crates explain themselves and show where they will go.
- If you run out of hearts you stay in the same place, back at the lantern.
- On phones the game fills the whole screen, and the "turn your phone" screen shows a little phone turning.
