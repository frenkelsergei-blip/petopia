# PETOPIA 0.3.5 · Secrets of the windmill

- Foxy can dig up an old lever near the windmill. Pull it and a secret wall opens into the Windmill Cellar.
- The cellar is a challenge room: crumbling stones, a log raft and a stone gate that only stays open with something heavy on its plate. The prize is a golden airplane propeller toy part.
- The world feels alive: sparkling flowing water, drifting leaves, fireflies and light beams in the cave.
- A small map of the room you're in sits under its name; tap it for the full map.
- The Menu button is now a little wooden sign.
