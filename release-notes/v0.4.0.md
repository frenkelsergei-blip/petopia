# PETOPIA 0.4.0 · Foxy's kitchen, shop and a friendlier Green Meadows

- Choose what Foxy eats: kibble is always free, and berries, apple slices, fish treats, honey cookies and carrots come from the shop.
- Foxy has favourites (fish and berries) and is not keen on carrots.
- A new shop for food, toys and the blue collar.
- New loading screen: a Play now button when ready and a What's new button with the version.
- The adventure bag fits on one screen; tap a snack and Foxy eats it right there in the world.
- The map shows the path between places, with soft drifting clouds over places you have not explored.
- The Old Tower inside is one continuous stone tower with windows, torches and ivy.
- Birds, butterflies, bunnies, a squirrel and a duckling live in Green Meadows.
- The bridge crank works both ways.
- Start Green Meadows again from Pause and Settings (tap twice to confirm).
