# PETOPIA 0.10.6 · World touch-ups

- Signs carry a paper hint badge in the game's style, centred on the board.
- The bridge wheel is new: a wooden stand with a round wheel that really turns.
- Windmill Cellar: walk up to the button and Foxy hops on it to hold the gate open for you.
- Flags, signs and plants no longer have odd dark shadows.
- No more pink edges in the cellar, cave and tower.
- More coins to find in every place.
