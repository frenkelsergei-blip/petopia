# PETOPIA 0.7.0 · Paper theater

The whole game moves to the handcrafted paper-cut look (A55).

- The explorer and Foxy are paper puppets with jointed parts, soft contact shadows and warm shading; Foxy's size follows his age.
- Green Meadows, River Crossing, Hidden Cave and the Old Tower are paper dioramas: depth layers, clouds and sun hanging on twine, a soft close-up layer of leaves in front, leafy paper ledges, paper props, doors, critters and Moss.
- New paper controls and HUD: joystick, Find/Use/Jump with context icons, status card, coins, room-name plaque.
- Main menu, bag, pet details, shop, settings and the other screens are built from the paper concepts with live text.
- New My Pet room (a paper treehouse): the explorer fills the food and water bowls, Foxy eats, drinks, bathes in the tub with bubbles and a duck, shakes dry and sleeps curled in his bed.
- Paper adoption screen, adventure map, garden for Catch the Ball and picnic result picture.

Validation: all 15 automated suites pass (systems, scenes, graybox, cooperation, smoothness, trail tools, mobile, art polish, main menu, adoption, home growth, web incl. the 220 MiB image budget, care, UI layout, world). Pause & Settings layout from P15, the room makeover shop and decoration spots are not in this version.
