# PETOPIA 0.4.3 · Friendly critters, clear gates and a map that shows the way

- The joystick stops the moment you let go (it could keep walking after a room change).
- Double jump goes its full height every time, with a little puff of air.
- New friendly critters in every place: bats in the Old Tower, crystal crabs in the Hidden Cave, frogs at the River Crossing and bumblebees at the Forest Gate. Hop on them to make them dizzy.
- Each path to a new place has its own gate with a picture of where it leads (cottage, windmill, river), and a big name ribbon shows when you arrive.
- The map shows your next goal with a bouncing star and a "Next" line.
- The windmill lever works both ways: pull again to close the secret wall.
- Birds fly forward across the sky instead of being dragged back when you walk back.
