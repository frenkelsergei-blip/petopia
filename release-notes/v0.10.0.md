# PETOPIA 0.10.0 · Tamagotchi pet room, upright Foxy

- My Pet is now a classic Tamagotchi: Foxy stands big in the middle of the treehouse and you care for it with four big buttons, Feed, Play, Bath and Sleep. A thought bubble shows what Foxy needs; tap Foxy to give it a pat.
- Foxy stands and moves upright like a little person, in the room and in the adventure.
- Every button has the same light, soft shadow.
- Catch the Ball: Foxy holds just one ball, and the power bar shows the catch zone as a soft golden glow.
