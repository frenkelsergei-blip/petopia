# PETOPIA 0.3.9 · Keys, snacks and a real tower

- Find things on the way: apples and fish snacks give Foxy energy, magic carrots give you one big jump, and a golden key opens the locked door at the top of the Old Tower. A clue note tells you where a secret lever is.
- Foxy no longer gets left behind: if Foxy falls far behind, Foxy hops right back to you.
- The Old Tower is now a proper round tower covered in ivy.
- Signs show a little light bulb; walk up and tap Read for the hint.
- The thorny gate to Mystic Forest says it opens in a future adventure, and you get a "Green Meadows complete!" moment when you find all robot parts and finish the picnic.
