# PETOPIA 0.2.7 · First pass toward the concept art

- Adventure world: new layered earth banks replace the big repeating boulder pattern; large oaks, birches, flowering bushes, fences and rocks stand behind the trail; ferns and flowers grow in front of the bank edge. Both adventures use them.
- Adventure controls and top bar follow the concept: level chip, run coins, bag and settings, a live goal chip, and wooden Find / Use / Jump tiles with icons. The round movement stick stays.
- World map: dotted trail between stops, explorer marker, level card with a preview picture and companion, locked Sunny Beach and Mystic Forest cards.
- Pause & Settings: art card with Resume / My Pet / Main menu beside settings with On/Off switches and left/right steppers; game stays visible behind.
- Results: "A picnic, together!" card with reward rows, total, new memory and My Pet / Play again; the celebration picture stays visible on phones.
- Screens now size by real screen points: phone landscape and tablets were drawing in physical pixels (too small), and desktop buttons shrank as the window grew.

- Buttons and menus: one wooden frame and one layout rule everywhere. Icons sit at the same inset and are vertically centred, text is centred, rivets are a fixed size and never touch labels, square tiles (care actions, Find/Use/Jump, Menu) have the icon centred above the label. Pet details rows, reward rows, wallet and chips use the same rule; small chips drop a second line rather than overflow.
- One look for every control type: same heights and text sizes per role, thin crisp wooden borders on small buttons, one square style for Settings/Menu/Close/Bag, flat name tags instead of tiny wooden frames, rounded meter bars, centred switches.
- Care drawer and Menu no longer blur the room; the drawer header follows the concept.
- New approved UI kit on every screen: carved wooden panels, gold and cream pill buttons, round icon buttons, soft tiles and tags, round map stops with Foxy on the current one, and round wooden action buttons in adventures. Nothing is stretched out of shape.

New art made with Codex image generation is a review candidate, not final approval. Physical phone check still needed.
