# PETOPIA 0.4.2 · Everything you find goes in your bag

- Crates come along: leaving a room puts the crates you placed back in your bag, ready for the next place (a crate on a pressure plate stays to hold the gate open).
- Apples and fish you find go into the bag as snacks; give them to Foxy whenever you like.
- Jump carrots wait in the bag; tap one when you need a big jump.
- New "Found on the way" row in the bag: jump carrots, the Sky Feather and the golden key.
- The Sky Feather leaves its stand once Foxy has it, so it is clear you collected it.
- Clearer quest steps: "Find Moss's picnic basket at the top of the Old Tower", "Bring the picnic basket to Moss".
- Old Tower: safe steps on the far side of the thorns lead back onto the climb if you fall.
- The room name and hearts at the top no longer overlap after changing rooms.
