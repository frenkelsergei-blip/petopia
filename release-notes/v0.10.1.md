# PETOPIA 0.10.1 · Foxy has feelings

- Foxy shows how it feels: hungry, tired or sad when its needs are low.
- It eats the food you choose, held in its paws, and loves its favourites but pulls a face at foods it dislikes.
- When worn out it curls up in its bed by itself and wakes up when rested.
- After Catch the Ball it is proud of its catches, or a bit down.
- Foxy is sharper, with a soft shadow on the rug.
- Every button has the same light, even shadow.
- On phones held upright, the menu, food and decorate boards fit the screen.
