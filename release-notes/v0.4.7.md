# PETOPIA 0.4.7 · Music and sound

- Music for every place: the main menu, My Pet's cozy room, the Green Meadows, the echoing Hidden Cave and Old Tower, and a bouncy Catch the Ball tune.
- Sounds for everything: buttons, coins, chests, doors, levers, the bridge, crates, pickups, hearts, lanterns and friendly critters.
- The child jumps, lands, runs and says "ouch"; Foxy yips, sniffs, digs, munches, laps water, splashes, shakes, purrs and snores.
- New Music switch in Settings (next to Sound).
- The Sky Feather stays on view: once you take it, it rises from its stand and flies into your bag, and the stand stays, empty and softly glowing. The cave sign changes too.
