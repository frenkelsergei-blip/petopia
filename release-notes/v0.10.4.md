# PETOPIA 0.10.4 · Smoother play

- Catch the Ball: Foxy stays on screen, the ball flies in a softer arc, Foxy hands it back into the boy's hand, and the boy holds it in his hand while running.
- Every button has a clear, soft shadow all around it.
- A bigger joystick that stops and returns to the middle as soon as you let go.
- Space no longer opens Settings by accident.
- Cleaner buttons: icons only, no labels.
- Animals look smoother, with softer, better-placed shadows.
- The coin counter grows to fit big numbers.
- Tips appear only when Hints are on in Settings, centred on screen.
