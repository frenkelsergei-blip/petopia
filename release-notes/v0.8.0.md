# PETOPIA 0.8.0 · Torn-paper world

- Green Meadows, River Crossing, Forest Gate, Hidden Cave, Old Tower and Windmill Cellar rebuilt in the torn-paper style of the new concepts: each place has its own layers, ground and props made from its concept, with clear depth between layers and a soft foreground layer that moves.
- The explorer and Foxy are new: soft paper-collage characters drawn frame by frame (run, jump, land, dig, happy), sized like in the concepts.
- Every picture in the world comes from the new style (checked automatically).

Still in progress: menus in the new style, level layouts redesigned as connected Metroidvania areas, more animation polish.
