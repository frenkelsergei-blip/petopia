# PETOPIA 0.13.0 · Meadow Fields in layers

- Meadow Fields is a new painted place you explore up and down: drop into the secret burrow to a glowing root chamber with a treasure, climb the ladder up the windmill cliff, go up the ivy to the windmill, cross the mossy log bridge to the sun gate.
- Foxy digs up the old lever on the cliff; it opens the sun gate to the Windmill Cellar.
- The robot-arm chest waits on the paw ledge under the big tree: you see it, but need Foxy's Boost (or a magic carrot) to reach it.
- Climbing: hold Up or Down at a ladder or vine (joystick up/down on phones). Foxy climbs right behind you.
