# PETOPIA 0.12.0 · One look for every screen

- Every menu is rebuilt from the new approved designs: the same header (X, leafy title, coins only where they matter), the same cards, buttons, tabs and fonts on My Pet, Feed, Menu, Details, Shop, Games, Collection, Decorate, Settings, Pause, Bag, the map and Adventures.
- Adventures: Green Meadows with its real map and robot parts; Sunny Beach and Mystic Forest are locked until you finish it.
- Catch the Ball: new look with a gold-centre power meter, a "Great game!" pop-up with stars, soft ground shadows under the boy and Foxy, Foxy carries the ball back in its paws, and the boy's boots are no longer cut.
- Settings fit on one screen (no scrolling); the extra touch options are under "More".
- Sign tips appear in the middle of the screen; place names show on one leafy banner.
