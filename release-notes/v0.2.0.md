# PETOPIA0.2.0 · A quieter home and visible care

The home now follows the selected quiet-room → care-drawer flow. Keep the room visible, tap **Care for Foxy** for six activities, or open **Menu** for Details, Adventures, Shop, Decorate, Collection, Mini games, Settings and Main menu.

- Water means drinking from a bowl; Clean remains bathing. Feed, drink, pet, wash and rest show room behavior before saved effects. Leaving unfinished care spends no owned meal. Native idle/contact poses and grounded sleeping furniture.
- Home supports portrait and landscape. The Care drawer opens below in portrait and beside the pair in landscape. The same live room softly blurs behind overlays; the Details portrait remains sharp.
- Adventures ask to rotate a phone to landscape, pause held input while upright and resume the same run. Round stick, independent action buttons, Bag and settings retained.
- Current two adventures, owned meals/toy placement, collection memories, fetch and real completion rewards remain connected. Future levels stay unavailable.
- Shared coin and illustrated controls; improved native icon/text proportions. Original rocky river and crossing retained.
