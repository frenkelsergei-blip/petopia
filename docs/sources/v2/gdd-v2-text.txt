PETOPIA V2 — Complete Game Design Document
Existing V1 and V2 vision
V1 already includes pet selection, several pet characters, pet growth, feeding, bathing, ball throwing, play
interactions, and an adventure world. Preserve these features. V2 extends the existing project with connected
2D Metroidvania exploration, parallax scenery, toy-part collection, Toy Builder, on-foot Pet Racing, Pet Chef,
and a broader Game Center. All activities must share persistent pet, currency, and inventory data.
Core loop
Care for pet at home -> explore with pet or choose a minigame -> earn coins, ingredients and toy parts -> cook,
craft, customize -> feed and play with pet -> strengthen bond and unlock skills -> explore further. Each mode
should affect another mode.
Pet care and real-time growth
Persistent attributes: hunger, thirst, hygiene, energy, happiness, health and bond. Feeding, bathing, sleeping,
playing and training use interactive animations. Pets can become mildly unwell when neglected and always
recover through accessible care; no permanent death. Time-based development: baby (days 1–7), child (days
8–30), teen (days 31–60), adult (day 61+), adjustable after playtesting. Offline decay is capped; children must
not be pressured to check in frequently. Bond affects reactions, tricks, greetings and cooperative abilities.
Characters and abilities
Long-term pet roster: Foxy (hidden objects), Mochi (narrow spaces), Bubbles (water), Ember (fire), Sprout
(vines), Frosty (ice), Bolt (machines), Luna (secrets), Poppy (jump), Pebble (shield), Sunny (glide), Cloudy
(clouds). MVP: Foxy, Ember and Bubbles. Shared animations: idle, run, jump, fall, eat, drink, wash, sleep,
happy, sad, sick, recover, play, ability. Child explorer: idle, run, jump, interact, celebrate, pet and carry. Use
consistent pixel density.
Metroidvania connected world
Use a connected graph of 2D rooms rather than only independent levels. Discover locked routes, gain pet
abilities, revisit earlier locations, find secrets and toy parts. Keep child-friendly objective markers, maps,
checkpoints, hints and shortcuts. Avoid blocking the main story for weeks behind real-time pet growth. Each
region combines platforming, exploration, pet-assisted puzzles and optional challenge rooms.
Green Meadows
Home -> Meadow Fields -> River Crossing -> Hidden Cave -> Old Tower -> Forest Gate. First playable vertical
slice: walk/jump, pet companion follow, chest containing a toy part, one ability gate, a secret and return route.
Visuals: bright sky, distant mountains, rolling hills, village, windmill, trees, river, bridges, grass, flowers and
foreground leaves.
Mystic Forest
Enchanted paths, hanging vines, giant trees, glowing mushrooms, crystals, forest village and hidden chambers.
Challenges: grow vine bridges, light puzzles, hidden doors and companion abilities. Visuals: deep layered
canopy, mist, drifting fireflies, animated plants and glowing crystals.
Robot Factory

Connected factory rooms with conveyors, circuits, switches, gears, power stations, moving platforms and robot
workshop. Collect robot heads, arms, wheels and mechanical components. Use industrial silhouettes, distant
buildings, pipes, blinking control panels and independently animated gears.
Parallax background architecture
Use separate transparent layers, not a flattened image. Example scroll factors: sky/clouds 0.05, mountains
0.10, hills 0.20, distant structures 0.35, midground trees 0.60, terrain 1.00, foreground vegetation 1.20. Godot 4:
Parallax2D nodes with scroll_scale and repeat_size when textures tile seamlessly. Environmental motion
(water, fireflies, gears, foliage) is separate animation. Verify seams, pixel snapping, vertical camera movement,
contrast and mobile performance.
Environment art production
Create for each world: sky strips, mountain silhouettes, distant vegetation, midground structures, foreground
foliage, terrain TileSet (edges/corners/slopes), bridges, doors, chests, platforms, switches, props, animated
effects and collision data. Keep source layered files plus exported transparent PNGs. Concept art is inspiration,
not production-ready sprites. Establish tile size, base resolution, pivot, palette, naming and sprite scaling before
mass production.
Toy collection loop
Adventure chests, secrets, puzzles and minigame rewards grant identifiable toy parts. Inventory records each
unique part; blueprint displays progress such as Robot 6/10 and hints for missing parts. Children can assemble
already collected pieces immediately and save partial progress. Completed toys become interactive items in the
pet room.
Toy Builder mechanics
2D workshop: central blueprint with numbered slots, side tray of collected parts, progress indicator and hint
button. Drag a part near its correct slot -> snap, animate and persist; incorrect drops return gently. Provide
tap-select/tap-target alternative. Easy 3–5 pieces, normal 6–10, advanced 10–15; no rotation initially. Initial
toys: robot 10 parts, airplane 7, car 6. Generic data-driven assembly engine: toy_id, part_id, PNG texture, slot
position, snap radius, order/dependencies, installed state, completion reward. Finished toy can be used with
pet.
Pet Racing mechanics
Entirely 2D side-view on-foot pet auto-runner, NOT karts. Four pets compete; player times jumps, power-ups
and special ability. Magnet attracts coins; speed boost increases speed; shield protects against obstacle; pet
ability may double-jump or glide. 45–90 second races. Tracks: Green Meadows Circuit, Mystic Forest Dash,
Robot Factory Sprint. MVP: one track, four racers, countdown, rank indicator, coins, finish screen and persistent
rewards. Keep AI competitors fair and readable.
Pet Chef mechanics
Choose recipe -> obtain ingredients -> drag/chop -> stir -> timing challenge -> plate -> store cooked meal ->
feed pet in home. Simple 2D sprite animations and forgiving touch interactions. Start with three recipes and two
preparation mechanics. Meals change hunger and happiness, while preferences influence pet reactions.
Game Center catalog
Other games: Fruit Match, Memory Paws, Catch the Treats, Fishing Friends, Bubble Pop, Pet Hide & Seek,
Treasure Dig, Puzzle Pieces, Garden Grow, Color Splash, Pet Bowling, Shape Sorter, Maze Explorer, Dance
Party. Each standalone Godot scene uses shared launch, pause, result, reward and save APIs. Target 1–3
minute sessions with child-friendly difficulty and participation rewards.

Godot 4 implementation
Suggested autoloads: GameState, SaveManager, PetManager, InventoryManager, CurrencyManager,
QuestManager, SceneRouter. Scene families: Home, PetRoom, AdventureRoom, Player, PetCompanion,
GameCenter, PetRacing, PetChef, ToyBuilder. Data definitions: PetSpecies, Item, ToyBlueprint, Recipe,
RaceTrack, WorldRoom, Ability, RewardTable. Version save data and migrate V1 safely. Signals: pet_changed,
item_collected, toy_completed, minigame_finished, ability_unlocked.
UI and accessibility
Home shows pet mood/needs and clear buttons for Care, Adventure, Game Center and Shop. Adventure
shows objective, coins, ability and map. Racing shows position, countdown, pickups and progress. Toy Builder
shows blueprint, numbered slots and parts. Chef shows recipe and current action. Support touch, mouse and
keyboard, large hit areas, readable pixel fonts, accessible non-drag alternatives, reduced-motion and forgiving
feedback.
V2 production roadmap
1 Audit V1 scenes/assets/save data. 2 Implement layered Green Meadows and room transitions. 3 Add toy
parts, chests and persistent inventory. 4 Implement generic Toy Builder with one robot. 5 Implement 2D Pet
Racing with one track. 6 Implement Pet Chef with three recipes. 7 Extend Mystic Forest and Robot Factory with
ability gates. 8 Add Game Center catalog, polish, onboarding, QA and performance tests.
Acceptance criteria
A child can select an existing V1 pet, feed and bathe it, explore Green Meadows with it, find a toy part, return to
the workshop, assemble part of a toy, play a 2D race, cook a meal, feed the meal to the pet and relaunch with
all progress intact. Pet care, health and growth remain functional. All new systems are modular and do not
regress V1.
Developer implementation prompt
Extend the existing Godot 4 PETOPIA V1 project; first inspect its current scenes, autoloads, pet data, save
format and animation conventions. Preserve working pet selection, feeding, bathing, ball throwing, growth and
adventure. Add connected 2D Metroidvania exploration with Parallax2D multi-layer environments, collectible toy
parts, data-driven Toy Builder with partial assembly, on-foot four-pet racing with magnet/boost/shield, and
interactive Pet Chef that creates inventory meals. Integrate all modes with the existing pet state, coins and
persistence. Deliver testable milestones, sample content and a QA checklist. Do not introduce 3D racing or
replace the existing pet simulation.
